Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Short version: Threefold uses the information you provide to operate the service, deliver messages, remember your preferences and interactions, and improve the experience. We do not sell your personal information or share your SMS opt-in information with third parties for their marketing.

Information we may collect

When you use Threefold, we may collect your phone number, name or chosen display name, message content, timestamps, opt-in and opt-out status, preferences, responses to activities, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to deliver and personalize Threefold messages, respond to messages you send, remember program progress and preferences, schedule requested reminders, maintain consent records, prevent misuse, troubleshoot the service, and improve the experience.

Service providers

Threefold may use trusted service providers to deliver messaging, host software and data, and provide automated language or AI capabilities. These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to Threefold and subject to their applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

SMS data

Mobile information, SMS consent records, and opt-in data will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with vendors that help us provide the messaging service, or when required by law, to protect users, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Couples and privacy

Two people participating as a couple may each interact with Threefold from separate phone numbers. Unless an experience is clearly identified as shared, one participant's private messages are not automatically disclosed to the other participant. Features that intentionally share content between partners should make that sharing clear before the content is shared.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the pilot, maintain required messaging and consent records, resolve problems, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of information that is not required to be retained by contacting us.

Your choices

You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for messaging assistance. You may also contact us to ask questions about your information or request an appropriate correction or deletion.

Children

Threefold is intended for adults age 18 and older and is not designed for children.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No online or messaging system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as Threefold develops. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
