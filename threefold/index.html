Threefold SMS Program

Threefold is currently operating as a limited pilot. Adults may opt in by texting JOIN to +1 (903) 609-0465 or by accepting an invitation that clearly discloses the SMS program. Messages may include relationship prompts, date-night ideas, reminders, interactive activities, program updates, and replies to messages you initiate.

Example: Threefold: Your next small adventure is ready. Want something fun, romantic, or unexpected tonight? Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time. Help: Reply HELP or email drbell@lonestarteacher.com. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

A relationship companion — not counseling

Threefold is a relationship-focused educational and recreational experience. It is not therapy, professional counseling, medical care, legal advice, or an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, contact local emergency services.
